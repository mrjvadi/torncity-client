# Art style — "Tehran dusk"

A warm, modern, slightly toy-like city seen from above: clean rounded shapes,
Persian tile accents (turquoise domes, saffron and lapis bands, arched
windows), soft light from the upper left, and a night-blue interface that lets
the city's colours carry the screen.

All art is original and generated by code in `art_src/`, so it can be re-run
at other sizes, restyled from one palette, or replaced slot by slot.

## Palette

One file, `assets/art/palette.json`, feeds both the art generators and the
runtime theme (`AppTheme`). Change a value, re-run the generators, and art and
UI move together.

| Role | Names |
|---|---|
| Interface | `ink` (deepest background), `night` (screen), `panel`, `panel_hi`, `line`, `text`, `text_dim` |
| Accents | `turquoise` (primary, «Persian blue-green»), `saffron` (money, highlights), `lapis` (bank, civic), `pomegranate` (health, alerts), `rose`, `violet`, `leaf`, `sky` |
| Materials | `sand`, `brick`, `stone`, `steel`, `olive`, `slate`, `earth`, `asphalt`, `road`, `grass`, `water` |

Each concept owns a colour so it reads before its shape: energy and cash —
saffron and leaf; health — pomegranate; bank — lapis/sky; hunger — brick;
sleep — violet; stress — rose; happiness — leaf; XP — turquoise.

## Shape language

- **Icons** (64 × 64): filled, no outlines, 4 px safe margin, corner radii 4–8,
  stroke details 5 px with round caps; two tones per icon (a base and a
  lighter or darker partner) plus one highlight stroke from the upper left.
- **Avatars** (256 × 256): the twelve presets of `life.yml` (fox, lion, owl,
  wolf, cat, eagle, tulip, rocket, gem, crown, panda, dove), each on its own
  colour disc with a radial light and an inner ring, clipped to the circle.
- **Places** (256 × 256 art space): isometric 2:1 lots, 100 × 100 world units,
  lot centre at (128, 200) of the canvas, nothing below y = 253. Light from the
  upper left: tops lightest, down-left faces in light, down-right faces in
  shade. Landmarks: a clock tower and fountain (city centre), domes and striped
  stalls (bazaar), glass towers and a columned bank (business district), a
  domed hall with a clock (university), a gold-domed city hall with a flag, a
  blue police station with a beacon and patrol car, a hospital with a helipad,
  cross and ambulance, a glass-vaulted train station with a train, a bus
  terminal with a canopy and a bus, an airport with a tower and an airliner,
  a saw-tooth factory with chimneys and containers, a barn, silo and fields,
  houses around a crossroads, a park with a pond and gazebo, and barracks with
  a fence, watchtower and parade ground.
- **UI**: panels are drawn in code (`GlowPanel`): a layered soft shadow, a
  vertical gradient, a 2 px top highlight, a hairline border, an optional
  accent line. Buttons have a darker lip that disappears when pressed and a
  squash-and-spring on press. Bars are glossy capsules; rings are radial
  gauges. Reference 9-patch SVGs of the same look are in `assets/art/ui/`.

## Two pipelines, one set of descriptions

`art_src/places.py` describes every place with a small vocabulary — boxes,
gable roofs, cylinders, domes, cones, window grids, trees, lamps, flags,
vehicles, puffs and discs.

1. **Vector** (`python3 art_src/gen_art.py`): `art_src/iso.py` draws that
   vocabulary as layered SVG with per-face shading, gradients on round forms,
   highlights and cast shadows. Standard library only.
2. **3D render** (`art_src/blender/render_places.py`): `art_src/blender/iso3d.py`
   builds the same vocabulary as Blender meshes (framed, glossy windows;
   smooth domes; spherical trees; real wheels), grouped per material, and
   renders them with Cycles: an orthographic camera at 30° matched to the SVG
   projection (heights scaled by cos 45°/cos 30°), a warm key sun with soft
   shadows, a cool fill, sky ambient and path-traced ambient occlusion, at 2×
   (512 px) on a transparent background. The palette is kept exact by the
   "Standard" view transform.

`manifest.json` tries the rendered PNG first and falls back to the SVG, so
either output — or an artist's file — fills a slot.

The walking character is rendered the same way
(`art_src/blender/render_character.py`): a procedural figure posed with a sine
walk cycle, 4 isometric directions × 8 frames plus idle frames, packed into
`assets/art/character/walker.png` with a `walker.json` layout. Without it the
map draws a vector stand-in.

## Replacing art (artists, CC0 packs)

Every image is reached through a slot in `assets/art/manifest.json`:

| Slot | Default file | Size / anchor |
|---|---|---|
| `icon/<name>` | `icons/<name>.png` or `.svg` | square; drawn at 24–84 px |
| `place/<code>` | `places/render/<code>.png`, else `places/<code>.svg` | 256² art space (any multiple); lot centre at (128, 200) |
| `avatar/<code>` | `avatars/<code>.png` or `.svg` | square, round subject |
| `item/<code>` | `items/<code>.png` or `.svg` | square |
| `brand/logo`, `brand/splash`, `brand/icon` | `brand/*.svg` | 512², 720 × 1280, 256² |
| walker | `character/walker.png` + `walker.json` | frames, rows and feet anchor in the JSON |

Drop a PNG with the same name next to an SVG and it wins; no code changes. A
Kenney-style CC0 isometric pack fits the place slots if its tiles are scaled
so a lot's diamond is 200 × 100 px per 256 px of width.

## Motion

Eased tweens everywhere (back-out for entrances, elastic for button release,
cubic for numbers rolling), staggered list entrances, a sliding tab pill,
particle bursts (coins on income, sparkles on arrival), a hop and pulse when
the player arrives, drifting clouds, water shimmer and traffic on the map,
parallax mountains, and a day/night tint that follows Tehran time.
